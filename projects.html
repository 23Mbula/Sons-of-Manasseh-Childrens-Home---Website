Our Projects

Building a stronger future together.

From permanent facilities to sustainable income-generating projects, our work is focused on creating a safe, stable and supportive environment for children.

Where We Are Today

Creating a safe and stable home for every child.

We currently operate in semi-permanent structures and desire to build permanent facilities to provide a stable and safe environment for our children.

These projects are an important part of strengthening the home and creating an environment where children can learn, grow and thrive.

Existing Projects

Projects already making a difference.

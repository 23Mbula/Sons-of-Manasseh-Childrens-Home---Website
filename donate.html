Questions?

Giving FAQs

Who receives my donation?

Sons of Manasseh Children's Home is a registered Children's Charitable Institution (CCI) in Kenya. Gifts support the care, education and protection of the children and the development of the home.

Can I give items instead of money?

Yes. Food, clothing, books, toiletries, bedding and other supplies are welcome. Please contact us first so we can let you know what is needed most.

Can my church, school or company give as a group?

Yes. We welcome groups and organisations. Contact us to talk about a gift, a project or a partnership.

How do I know my gift was received?

Send us a short message with your name and the date of your gift, and we will be glad to confirm it and say thank you.

I have another question about giving.

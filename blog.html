Skills & Talents
Life Skills at SMCH

Little Hands, Big Skills: Knitting, Crochet and Beadwork

At Sons of Manasseh Children's Home, learning does not stop when the school day ends. Alongside their studies, our children are taught practical skills that build patience, creativity and confidence, skills they can carry with them for life.

Knitting and crochet

With a ball of yarn and a pair of needles, a few quiet hours turn into something real. The children learn to keep their stitches even, to be patient, and to start again when a row goes wrong. A piece of work grows one stitch at a time, and the pride on a child's face when they hold it up is something to see.

Beadwork

Beadwork is another favourite. Tiny beads in red, black, green and white, the colours of the Kenyan flag, are threaded one by one into bracelets and keepsakes. It takes focus, a steady hand and a good eye for colour, and it is lovely to watch the children share tips and help one another.

Why these skills matter

These activities teach far more than crafts. Children learn to finish what they start and to see that their hands can make something beautiful and useful. Skills like these can also open doors to future income, which is the same thinking behind our Ankara project, where sewing helps build skills and income for the home.

“Every stitch and every bead teaches patience.”

Faith & Spirit
Spiritual Life at SMCH

Spiritual Nourishment: Feeding the Heart as Well as the Body

Sons of Manasseh began as a simple Saturday outreach that shared the Gospel and a small meal with vulnerable children. Almost two decades later, we still believe that every child needs both: good food for the body and nourishment for the spirit.

Many children come to us after loss or hardship. Knowing that they are loved, that they have a purpose and that their story is not over gives them something no meal or school uniform can: hope.

Stories, devotions and reading

Faith comes alive when it is shared in ways children can enjoy. Our children read Bible storybooks and devotionals, such as The Jesus Storybook Bible and Max Lucado's One God One Plan One Life, a 365-day devotional for students. Quiet reading time, questions and conversation help them grow in understanding and in character.

Growing in character

Spiritual nourishment shapes how children treat one another: with kindness, honesty, respect, gratitude and forgiveness. These are the values we pray will guide them long after they leave the home.

“A child who knows they are loved can believe in a brighter future.”

We are grateful to every church, partner and well-wisher who prays with us and stands with us. If you would like to be part of this journey, get involved

Celebrations
Sweet Moments at SMCH

Life Is Sweeter With Cake

Let us be honest: at Sons of Manasseh Children's Home, we love cake. Not a little. A lot.

Cake has a special talent. It can turn an ordinary Saturday into a celebration, a graduation into a party, and a quiet afternoon into the best memory of the week. The moment a cake appears, voices get louder, smiles get wider and everyone is suddenly best friends.

Mother's Day, birthdays, Christmas and graduations have all had their cake moment here, and we have the pictures to prove it.

Our unofficial cake rules

  • Everyone gets a slice, and the pieces must be fair.
  • Sharing is not optional. It is the best part.
  • Icing is its own food group.
  • Nobody leaves the table until every crumb is accounted for.

More than just dessert

Behind the laughter is something that matters. A cake says: today is worth celebrating, and so are you. Whether it marks a birthday, a graduation, a good report card or Christmas, a shared cake helps children feel seen, valued and part of a family.

“Cake is better when it is shared.”

Do you have a favourite cake? Tell us about it. We promise to take the topic very seriously. Get in touch

Sports at SMCH

More Than a Game: The SMCH Football Club

Football has become one of the activities that brings our children together. Through the SMCH Football Club, children have a space to play, build friendships, develop their skills, and enjoy being part of a team.

Training sessions give the children an opportunity to stay active while learning important values such as teamwork, discipline, commitment, and respect. Every training session and match is also a chance for the children to encourage one another and celebrate their progress.

Every player has their own kit with a number on the back, and the children wear them with pride.

Beyond the football pitch, the club creates an environment where children can discover their abilities and build confidence. Whether they are scoring a goal, making a great pass, defending, or cheering from the sidelines, every child has a role to play.

“Football gives us a chance to play, learn, and grow together.”

We look forward to seeing the SMCH Football Club continue to grow and create even more opportunities for our children to enjoy sport and develop their talents.

Christmas Holidays

Christmas at SMCH: A Season of Joy and Togetherness

Christmas is always a special time at Sons of Manasseh Children's Home. With schools closed and the children together, the holiday season gives everyone an opportunity to slow down, have fun, and enjoy time as a family.

The Christmas holidays are filled with games, music, laughter, good food, and activities that bring the children together. From decorating and preparing for Christmas to sharing meals and enjoying different activities, there is always something to look forward to.

For the children, some of the most memorable moments are often the simple ones — playing together, telling stories, reading Christmas storybooks in our reading room, singing, dancing, and spending time with friends and caregivers.

The holiday season is also a time to reflect on the year that has passed and look forward to the year ahead. It reminds us of the importance of family, love, gratitude, and community.

“Christmas is about creating memories, sharing joy, and celebrating together.”

We are grateful to everyone who makes these moments possible and helps create a warm and joyful Christmas experience for the children at SMCH.

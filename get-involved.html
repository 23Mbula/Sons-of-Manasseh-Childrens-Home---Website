Getting Involved

How can I volunteer?

Contact us to let us know about your interests, skills and availability. We will guide you on available opportunities and the next steps.

Can I volunteer as part of a group?

Yes. Groups, schools, churches, community organisations and other teams can contact us to discuss suitable ways to participate.

Can I volunteer by sharing my professional skills?

Yes. If you have professional, technical, creative or other useful skills, contact us and let us know how you would like to contribute.

Can my organisation partner with SMCH?

Yes. We welcome conversations with organisations, businesses, churches and other groups interested in exploring meaningful partnerships.

Can I support the home without volunteering?

Absolutely. You can donate, provide in-kind items, share your skills, organise a fundraiser or help spread awareness about our work.
